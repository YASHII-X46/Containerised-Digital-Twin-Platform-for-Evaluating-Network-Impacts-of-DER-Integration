Custom profiles

Upload any daily shape as CSV: one value per line, a single comma-separated line, or time and value rows. Any length and units are accepted; the shape is normalised to per-unit of peak. Load shapes become selectable as custom:name in the Profile column; PV and EV shapes are selected under Advanced modelling.
